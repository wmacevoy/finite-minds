Full siblings share 49.8% ± 3.6% of their genome (Visscher et al. 2006, PLoS Genetics)

So who is born? Not the same child. If the mother releases the same egg, only the father’s half changes, and the two possible children share about three quarters of their genes. That is closer than ordinary siblings. Chiang’s story calls them siblings, which is exactly right when the egg is different too: then they share about half, like any brother or sister. Either way it is a different person, and about half the time a different sex.
