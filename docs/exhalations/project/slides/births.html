Ten months after the air moves differently, every one of them is someone who otherwise would not exist.

UN World Population Prospects 2024: 132.1 million births in 2023

About 132 million babies were born in 2023, roughly 362,000 a day. The argument says each of them owes their existence to the exact path the air took ten months earlier. That includes every one of us. You exist because the weather on one particular day, and everything that shaped it, went exactly the way it did.
