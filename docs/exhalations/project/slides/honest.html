Being honest

What is settled, and what is still argued

Settled

  • Weather forgets its start in about two weeks
  • One shower or storm is enough to change it
  • A different sperm means a different person

Still argued

  • A breath on a sunny afternoon: theory says it grows
  • A breath on a calm night: it may simply fade
  • A single molecule: unproven either way

The air is full of such nudges. The only question is which one gets the credit.

An honest scorecard. Settled: weather forgets in about two weeks; a single storm-sized difference is enough; a different sperm is a different person. Argued: whether something as small as one breath counts. In turbulent daytime air, turbulence theory says yes, within an hour it reaches kilometre scale. On a calm night it might just fade. For a single molecule, physicists disagree. But notice: the atmosphere is full of these nudges all the time. Nobody doubts that the future is being reshuffled; the argument is only over whose breath did it.
