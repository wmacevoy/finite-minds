A story that took this seriously

“…within a month, the weather around the globe is different.”

Ted Chiang, “Anxiety Is the Dizziness of Freedom,” in Exhalation (2019)

In the story, a scientist compares weather reports from two versions of the world, split by a single photon. A month later they have nothing in common.

Ted Chiang’s story imagines a device that lets you talk to a parallel world that split from ours on one quantum event. A fictional atmospheric scientist, Peter Silitonga, uses it to compare weather reports. After a month they are seasonally right but otherwise unrelated. Chiang’s month is a generous round number; the science says about two weeks. Then Silitonga runs a second experiment, which is where this gets personal.
