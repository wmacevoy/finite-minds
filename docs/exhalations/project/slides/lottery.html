Here is the biology. A typical ejaculate holds around two hundred million sperm; the World Health Organization’s median is 210 million. Each one is genetically unique. The father’s 23 chromosome pairs can be sorted 8.4 million ways, and then about 26 crossovers per sperm cut and splice them further. Which sperm wins depends on tiny details of timing and motion, far finer than weather. So if the moment shifts, a different sperm wins, and a different person is conceived.
