Turbulence scaling (Aurell et al. 1996) · Selz & Craig (2015) · Zhang et al. (2019) · Judt (2018)

Here is the ladder. The air around you is full of swirls, and the small swirls feed the bigger ones. On a sunny afternoon a breath-sized difference reaches the scale of a kilometre in well under an hour. Once a single shower or storm is different, weather models show the difference reaching continent-sized systems in about ten days. Within about two weeks the weather everywhere on Earth is different. Honest footnote: the first step, from a breath to a kilometre, comes from turbulence theory rather than direct measurement. We will come back to that.
