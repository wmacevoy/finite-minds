Rippling forward

How far one difference reaches

Hours

A shower or storm turns out differently

~2 weeks

The weather everywhere is different

~10 months

Every newborn is a different person

~30 years

Different people become parents

~120 years

No one alive would be the same

Put it on one line. Within hours, a single shower or storm turns out differently. Within about two weeks, the weather everywhere is different. Ten months later, every baby born on Earth is a different person from the one who would have been born. A generation later, those different people are the parents, so their children are not even siblings of the alternatives. Their parents are different people. And after a little over a century, nobody alive would be the same.
