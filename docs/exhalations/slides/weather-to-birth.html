From weather to people

Weather changes when everything happens

Rain arrives ten minutes early

A bus runs late, a dinner runs long

A couple’s evening shifts by minutes

A different moment of conception

Nobody has to notice. A shift of seconds is enough, because of what happens next.

Once the weather is different everywhere, the timing of almost everything people do is different too. Rain comes earlier, a bus is late, a dinner runs long. Those shifts pass from person to person. For a couple trying to have a child, the moment of conception moves, even if only by seconds. Why would seconds matter? Because of the lottery on the next slide.
