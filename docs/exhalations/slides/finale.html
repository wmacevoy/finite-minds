Every breath

reaches everyone who will ever be born, and everything that breathes with them.

Come back to the people from the start, and widen the circle. The meditator, the worker, the runner, the kids. A parent with a newborn. A tree breathing out water from its leaves. A whale surfacing to exhale. A storm over a town. All of it shares one atmosphere, and every breath stirs it. The people who will be born next year, and every year after, depend on exactly how all of it moves. Take one more breath with me. That one counted too.
