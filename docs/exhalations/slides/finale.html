Every breath

reaches everyone who will ever be born, and everything that breathes with them.

Come back to the people from the start, and widen the circle. The meditator, the worker, the runner, the kids. A father with his newborn. A tree breathing out water into the morning mist. A whale surfacing to exhale. And a couple walking home in the rain, whose evening the weather just rearranged. All of it shares one atmosphere, and every breath stirs it. The people who will be born next year, and every year after, depend on exactly how all of it moves. Take one more breath with me. That one counted too.
